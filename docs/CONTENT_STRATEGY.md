# Content Strategy

The site should speak to senior AI decision-makers while still leaving a path for young technologists to feel inspired. The tone should be ambitious, precise, and useful.

## Positioning

Primary line:

> I build the infrastructure that makes AI agents reliable, observable, and enterprise-ready.

Support this with:

- Agent reliability.
- Evaluation systems.
- Observability.
- Guardrails.
- Enterprise workflows.
- Mission-grade autonomy.
- Long-term vision across agents, humanoids, and space.

## Route Intent

`/`

- Fast executive credibility.
- Clear thesis.
- Career arc.
- What Rahat builds.
- Speaking, writing, impact, and contact.

`/design-system`

- Internal/review artifact.
- Demonstrates reusable design language, animation, mission-control components, and Personal OS concepts.

`/landing-prototype`

- Prototype route for scroll-scrubbed hero and generated motion assets.
- Should remain separate until the homepage animation is production-ready.

`/projects`

- Sanitized case studies.
- Lead with agent observability and evaluation.
- Avoid confidential details.

`/writing`

- Redirects to `/vision#writing`.
- Writing should live inside Vision until there is enough published material to justify a standalone public tab.
- Topics should connect engineering depth to market and human impact.

`/speaking`

- Speaker bio, talk topics, abstracts, and inquiry call to action.
- Make conference organizers confident that Rahat has a clear point of view.

`/vision`

- Long-term thesis: AI agents, humanoids, space, and human potential.
- Should be concrete, not vague futurism.

`/os`

- Redirects to `/vision#rahat-os`.
- Rahat OS should be framed as a future direction inside Vision, not as a top-level product claim until the real system exists.
- The public/private/agents model must preserve a hard privacy boundary.

`/ai-multiverse`

- Retired from external navigation.
- Redirects to `/vision#rahat-os` so old links do not expose the outdated AI-tools experience.

`/journey`

- Add only when Rahat provides the actual personal life-story YouTube video.

## Voice

Use:

- Clear executive language.
- Specific technical nouns.
- First-person conviction when it helps.
- Calm ambition.
- Systems metaphors when they clarify the work.

Avoid:

- Empty visionary language.
- Too much career-coach framing.
- Overexplaining the UI.
- Claims that imply confidential shipped work.
- Treating the site like a generic personal brand funnel.

## Thought Leadership Pillars

AI agents:

- Reliability.
- Observability.
- Evaluation.
- Runtime governance.
- Human escalation.
- Enterprise adoption.

Humanoids:

- Embodied autonomy.
- Tool policy.
- World-state memory.
- Safety and supervision.
- Fleet learning.

Space:

- Delayed feedback.
- Mission assurance.
- Resilience.
- Autonomy under constraints.
- Auditability.

Human potential:

- Learning in public.
- Inspiring young technologists.
- Building from personal experience.
- Making advanced systems feel reachable.

## Public Safety Rules

- Sanitize Apple/internal work.
- Do not expose private personal data.
- Do not imply endorsements from employers, labs, or conferences unless verified.
- Keep unreleased agent/OS capabilities framed as vision or prototype until they exist.
